Hush Tab Mixer

A free browser extension that gives every tab its own volume slider.

  • Set each tab's volume: turn a YouTube video down to 30 percent while Hush plays at full volume.
  • Mute or unmute any tab, or mute everything except the tab you are on.
  • See what is playing and click a tab's name to jump straight to it.
  • Each tab keeps its volume when the page reloads.

Install it (Chrome, Edge or Brave)

  1. Download the zip above and unzip it. You will get a folder named hush-tab-mixer. Keep it somewhere you will not delete it, because the browser loads the extension from that folder.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (in Edge: edge://extensions, in Brave: brave://extensions).
  3. Turn on Developer mode (top right in Chrome; left side in Edge).
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the hush-tab-mixer folder.
  5. Click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar and pin Hush Tab Mixer. Click its icon any time to open the mixer.

Good to know

  • Which sites work: volume works on sites with standard audio and video players, such as YouTube, Spotify, Netflix and most music and video sites. A few sites (some games, browser settings pages) cannot be changed, but mute still works on them.
  • The permission warning: the browser says the extension can "read and change your data on all websites." It needs that to turn the volume down inside each page. It does not collect, store or send anything; it has no internet access of its own.
  • School computers: school-managed Chromebooks and computers often block installing extensions. If Load unpacked is missing or greyed out, the school has turned it off.
  • Updating: download the new zip, replace the folder, then click the reload arrow on the extension's card in chrome://extensions.
  • Firefox and Safari are not supported yet.
